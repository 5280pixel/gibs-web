Building the Future on a Legacy Brand
Team Perka + Miracle Truss Buildings
The evolution of Team Perka and Miracle Truss Buildings was not a traditional rebrand, merger, or brand consolidation.
It was a phased transition.
Team Perka had decades of credibility, manufacturing experience, and customer trust behind its building systems. Miracle Truss Buildings represented the opportunity to take that legacy, modernize the customer experience, and bring a renewed building brand to the forefront without losing the reputation and expertise that made the transition possible.
My role was to help guide that shift carefully.
Rather than abruptly replacing one brand with another, I helped create a gradual path in which Team Perka’s building line could move into the background as a legacy brand while Miracle Truss Buildings became the primary customer-facing identity.
The new Miracle Truss product line has continued to be fabricated by Team Perka’s Interlock Steel fabrication division since its relaunch. That connection remains an important part of the story. Miracle Truss is the brand customers see first, but the manufacturing history, technical knowledge, and production capabilities behind it are rooted in the Team Perka legacy.
My focus has been to make that relationship clear without creating confusion.
That work has required careful coordination across brand strategy, messaging, websites, sales materials, product literature, trade shows, proposals, advertising, and customer communications. Every piece needed to support Miracle Truss as the leading brand while still reinforcing the credibility of the Team Perka and Interlock Steel foundation behind it.
The challenge was not simply deciding which logo appeared first.
It was determining how to honor the past while building the future.
I worked closely with leadership and sales teams to establish language that connected the brands naturally. Miracle Truss needed to feel new, improved, and capable of standing on its own, while Team Perka needed to remain visible enough to reassure long-time customers and preserve the value of its history.
That balance continues today.
I still work along the fine line between advancing Miracle Truss as the primary brand and recognizing Team Perka as the legacy organization that helped make the product, manufacturing process, and customer promise possible.
The result has been a more modern and focused brand experience built on an established foundation. Miracle Truss now leads the customer conversation, while Team Perka and Interlock Steel continue to provide the history, manufacturing credibility, and operational strength behind it.
My Role
Brand transition strategy, creative direction, messaging architecture, website content, product positioning, sales enablement, trade show creative, customer communications, proposal strategy, print and digital collateral, and executive collaboration.
The Impact
A phased brand evolution that allowed Miracle Truss Buildings to move confidently into the foreground while preserving the trust, manufacturing heritage, and technical credibility of Team Perka and Interlock Steel.


Transforming a Fireworks Importer into a Full-Service Wholesale Partner
Schneitter Fireworks
When I began working with Schneitter Fireworks in 2016, the company had decades of industry knowledge, long-standing supplier relationships, and a respected place in the fireworks business. What it did not yet have was a complete, modern brand system or a clearly defined market position that reflected everything the company was capable of becoming.
As a result, the company had reached a crossroads: continue operating within an increasingly difficult and limiting model, or rethink how it presented itself, served its customers, negotiated with suppliers, and pursued growth.
What began as hands-on creative support evolved into a long-term leadership role in which I helped shape not only how Schneitter looked, but also how it thought about growth, customer service, supplier relationships, product access, and its place in the wholesale fireworks market.
One of the most important shifts was helping position Schneitter as a true one-stop shop for independent fireworks retailers.
Through its relationships with Chinese manufacturers, Schneitter could offer direct-ship containers to larger customers seeking full-container volume. At the same time, I helped champion the idea of mixed containers assembled from the company’s centrally located Missouri warehouse.
This created a more flexible solution for independent retailers.
Instead of committing to a full container from a single brand or manufacturer, customers could mix and match products across a broader selection. The model gave retailers greater variety, more control over inventory, and access to products that better matched their local markets.
That shift became more than an operational advantage. It became a central part of the company’s value proposition and customer messaging.
I was also instrumental in helping the company rethink how it approached supplier relationships.
At the time, Schneitter was too often accepting unfavorable treatment and operating from a position of limited leverage. I pushed for a more confident approach built on direct negotiation, face-to-face conversations, clearer expectations, and a willingness to walk away from suppliers that did not value the relationship.
That mindset shift mattered.
Rather than feeling locked into existing arrangements, the company began approaching manufacturers and brand partners with greater confidence. We became more willing to evaluate alternatives, negotiate from a stronger position, and move business toward suppliers that offered better products, service, terms, and long-term partnership.
This strengthened Schneitter’s ability to protect its margins, expand its product mix, and make decisions based on what was best for the company and its customers.
My creative work helped translate that broader strategy into a stronger wholesale brand through annual catalogs, direct-mail campaigns, price lists, digital advertising, social media, website development, product photography, sales materials, and customer communications.
The annual wholesale catalog became one of the most important expressions of the brand. I originally designed the catalog system from the ground up, establishing the structure, visual hierarchy, page architecture, product presentation, and overall creative direction.
Each new catalog is now built from the foundation of those original designs while being refreshed annually to reflect new products, changing priorities, and seasonal sales goals.
I followed the same approach across advertising, mail pieces, price books, and digital assets, creating repeatable design systems that could evolve without losing brand consistency.
Another major initiative was the creation of Danger Doll, Schneitter Fireworks’ private-label fireworks brand.
I developed the original brand identity, visual direction, logo, packaging concepts, and supporting marketing materials. Danger Doll gave Schneitter a distinctive product line it could own, promote, and grow independently within the broader wholesale market.
As Schneitter expanded, my role evolved with it.
In the early years, I personally handled nearly every aspect of the creative process, from concept development and design through production. As the company and its marketing needs grew, I transitioned from hands-on creator to trainer, teaching others how to use the systems, standards, and creative processes I had established.
Today, I serve primarily as the creative leader and final brand steward. I review, direct, refine, and approve the work created by the internal team, ensuring that catalogs, advertisements, digital materials, and customer communications remain consistent with the brand foundation I originally built.
That evolution is one of the achievements I am most proud of.
The goal was never to make the company dependent on me for every design. The goal was to build a creative system, develop the people around it, and create a brand strong enough to continue growing.
Over the course of the partnership, Schneitter Fireworks evolved from a business facing an uncertain future into a thriving national importer and wholesale partner. Its annual container volume grew to approximately three times its earlier level, supported by stronger branding, expanded product access, clearer customer communication, more confident supplier negotiations, and a more flexible wholesale model.
My Role
Contract Creative Director, brand strategy, business positioning, supplier-strategy input, catalog-system development, website direction, product photography, direct-mail strategy, digital marketing, advertising, Danger Doll private-label development, packaging direction, sales messaging, creative training, team development, and final brand approval.
The Impact
A modernized wholesale brand, a stronger one-stop-shop position for independent retailers, improved leverage with suppliers, greater customer access through direct-ship and mixed-container programs, the successful launch of the Danger Doll private label, and a scalable creative system that continues to guide the company’s catalogs, advertising, digital presence, and long-term growth.



Repositioning a Specialty Erector into a Full-Service Construction Brand
Veritas Systems Construction
Veritas Systems Construction began as a small, specialized crew focused primarily on erecting Team Perka building systems.
As Miracle Truss Buildings became the primary building brand and assumed responsibility for nationwide building sales, Veritas Systems Construction needed a clearer role, a stronger market position, and a brand story that reflected what the company had grown capable of delivering.
The opportunity was larger than a simple name change.
We relaunched Veritas Systems Construction as the expert local construction partner for Miracle Truss Buildings, while expanding its services beyond building erection to include Barn Fixer projects, renovation work, project coordination, and full general contracting throughout the region.
My role was to help define how that transformation would be presented to the market.
The company remained Veritas Systems Construction, or VSC, but the customer-facing brand evolved around the idea of Veritas Built.
VeritasBuilt.com became the company’s digital home. “That’s Veritas Built” became the brand promise: a way to communicate that the work was planned carefully, communicated clearly, and completed with precision, accountability, and pride.
The challenge was to create a brand that could carry several connected ideas at once.
Veritas needed to be recognized as:
    •    The local expert in erecting Miracle Truss Buildings
    •    The team behind Barn Fixer renovation and retrofit projects
    •    A capable general contractor for commercial, agricultural, residential, and community projects
    •    A trusted guide capable of helping customers move from early planning through construction
I led the repositioning through messaging strategy, website architecture, service development, content creation, visual direction, project storytelling, sales materials, and launch communications.
The new website and supporting brand materials were structured around the full customer journey. Rather than presenting Veritas as only an erection crew, the messaging showed how the company could support planning, site coordination, building erection, renovation, interior construction, and turnkey project delivery.
The phrase “That’s Veritas Built” became the thread connecting it all.
It described more than the finished structure. It represented the standard behind the work: honest communication, disciplined preparation, dependable execution, and accountability at every stage of the project.
The result was a clearer and more scalable brand position.
Veritas Systems Construction remained the company name and operating identity, while Veritas Built became the more memorable expression of what customers could expect from the company.
My Role
Brand repositioning, messaging architecture, website strategy, copywriting, service development, visual direction, project storytelling, launch communications, sales collateral, social content, and ongoing creative leadership.
The Impact
A specialized erection company was repositioned as a full-service local construction partner, with a clearer connection to Miracle Truss Buildings, a stronger market identity, and a brand platform capable of supporting building erection, Barn Fixer projects, general contracting, and future growth.
