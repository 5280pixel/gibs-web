## The challenge

[Veritas Systems Construction](https://veritasbuilt.com/) began as a small, specialized crew focused primarily on erecting Team Perka building systems.

As Miracle Truss Buildings became the primary building brand and assumed responsibility for nationwide building sales, Veritas needed a clearer role, a stronger market position, and a brand story that reflected what the company had grown capable of delivering.

The opportunity was larger than a name change. We relaunched Veritas Systems Construction as the expert local construction partner for Miracle Truss Buildings, while expanding its services beyond building erection to include Barn Fixer projects, renovation work, project coordination, and full general contracting throughout the region.

My role was to help define how that transformation would be presented to the market.

## My role

Brand repositioning, messaging architecture, website strategy, copywriting, service development, visual direction, project storytelling, launch communications, sales collateral, social content, and ongoing creative leadership.

## The approach

The company remained Veritas Systems Construction, or VSC, but the customer-facing brand evolved around Veritas Built.

VeritasBuilt.com became the company’s digital home. “That’s Veritas Built” became the brand promise: a way to say the work was planned carefully, communicated clearly, and completed with precision, accountability, and pride.

### Carry several connected ideas at once

The challenge was to create a brand that could hold several connected ideas without sounding scattered. Veritas needed to be recognized as:

- The local expert in erecting Miracle Truss Buildings
- The team behind Barn Fixer renovation and retrofit projects
- A capable general contractor for commercial, agricultural, residential, and community projects
- A trusted guide from early planning through construction

I led the repositioning through messaging strategy, website architecture, service development, content creation, visual direction, project storytelling, sales materials, and launch communications.

### Structure the brand around the full journey

The new website and supporting brand materials were structured around the full customer journey. Rather than presenting Veritas as only an erection crew, the messaging showed how the company could support planning, site coordination, building erection, renovation, interior construction, and turnkey project delivery.

### Make “That’s Veritas Built” the through-line

The phrase “That’s Veritas Built” became the thread connecting it all.

It described more than the finished structure. It represented the standard behind the work: honest communication, disciplined preparation, dependable execution, and accountability at every stage of the project.

## The impact

A specialized erection company was repositioned as a full-service local construction partner, with a clearer connection to Miracle Truss Buildings, a stronger market identity, and a brand platform that can support building erection, Barn Fixer projects, general contracting, and future growth.

Veritas Systems Construction remained the company name and operating identity. Veritas Built became the more memorable expression of what customers can expect from the company.
