# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, founders, and marketing leaders evaluating Paul Gibson II for senior marketing and creative roles. They want a clear sense of who he is, how he thinks, and whether his case studies prove fit — usually in a short visit from LinkedIn or a referral.

## Product Purpose

A portable marketing career portfolio that positions Paul as a solution-seeking creative marketing leader. Success means a visitor can grasp his WHY, scan three proof stories, and contact him via email or LinkedIn without friction.

## Brand Personality

Clear, sincere, practical. Confident without hype. People-first and solution-oriented.

## Anti-references

Generic purple-gradient SaaS landing pages. Dense resume dumps. Flashy agency portfolios that bury the person. Card grids of “skills pills.” Contact forms that replace a simple email CTA.

## Design Principles

- Brand first: Paul Gibson II and his presence should read as the product, not the nav.
- Clarity over clutter: one job per section; type and whitespace do the hierarchy.
- Proof through story: case studies carry weight as narratives with imagery, not feature lists.
- Easy to maintain: content lives in editable files so the site stays current without a CMS.
- Serve sincerity: copy and layout should feel approachable and trustworthy, matching his service-first WHY.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support `prefers-reduced-motion`. Ensure accent-on-cream and body text contrast meet readable thresholds. Keyboard-reachable nav and links; meaningful link text.
